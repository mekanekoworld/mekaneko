# Security

Found a bug that could put users or their funds at risk? Please tell us privately before posting it anywhere:
send a direct message to [@mekanekoworld on X](https://x.com/mekanekoworld), or open a ticket in #help on our
[Discord](https://discord.gg/42sGtf5Xx2) and ask a team member for a private channel. Do not include working
exploits in public channels.

We will never ask you for a seed phrase, a private key, or funds. Anyone who does is not us.
